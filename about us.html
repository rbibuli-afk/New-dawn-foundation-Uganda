Who We Are

New Dawn Foundation Uganda is a non-profit, community-focused organization established to promote mental health awareness, support individuals experiencing mental health challenges, and provide financial assistance to people who cannot afford essential medical care. The organization will use digital platforms and community partnerships to identify needs, mobilize resources, and connect beneficiaries with appropriate support services.

    • Our Inspiration

    We are inspired by the belief that everyone deserves access to mental health support and essential healthcare, regardless of their financial circumstances. We aim to reduce stigma, support people facing mental health challenges, and help those who cannot afford necessary medical care. Our work is driven by compassion, dignity, equity, and community support, with the goal of creating healthier, stronger, and more resilient communities.
