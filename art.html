Art Therapy

Colour outside the lines.

Art therapy is not about being skilled. It is about giving feelings a shape, colour, or pattern that can be seen without needing to explain them perfectly.

Try a prompt: draw how stress feels in your body, choose colours that match your mood, or make a chaotic abstract pattern and notice what it reveals about what you are carrying.

Creativity can be a healthy release valve. When words feel stuck, visual expression helps the emotion move through you instead of piling up inside.
