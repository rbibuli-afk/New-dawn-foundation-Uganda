Meditation Field

Step into stillness and breathe again.

Meditation is not about emptying your mind. It is about creating a small pocket of calm where your thoughts can slow down enough for you to feel grounded again.

Try this: inhale for four, exhale for six, and repeat for five rounds. Let your shoulders soften, feel the support beneath you, and notice one thing in the room that feels steady.

Even a short pause can reset the nervous system. It gives the mind permission to stop running for a moment and simply be present.
