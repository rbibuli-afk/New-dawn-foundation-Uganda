Music Suggestions

Let your mood guide the melody.

Music can soften stress, lift guilt, and help your emotions feel less heavy when they are difficult to name. This activity is for moments when you want a soundtrack that understands your current energy.

Try this: choose one song for each feeling you are carrying today — one for calm, one for clarity, and one for release. You do not need a perfect playlist; you only need a few tracks that make you feel seen.

Every song below is a recommended track you can open in YouTube Music. The direct links below make it easy to listen on your preferred device without depending on Spotify access.
