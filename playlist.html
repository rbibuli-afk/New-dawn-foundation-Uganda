Curate Your Feelings Playlist

Pick the songs that hold your emotions.

Sometimes the best way to understand what you feel is to build a playlist around it. This space helps you name the emotions you are carrying and match them with songs that gently reflect them.

How to use it: select songs for sadness, hope, calm, anger, joy, or exhaustion. You are not creating a “perfect” mood board — you are creating a compassionate archive of how you are feeling.

When a day feels messy, a playlist can become a ritual of self-recognition. It lets your feelings exist without shame and reminds you that all emotions deserve a place.
