Vent Out Space

Say everything you have been holding in.

This is a quiet space for pressure release. If your mind feels full, crowded, or exhausted, this page gives you room to put the weight down without being judged or edited.

Use this space honestly: write what is frustrating you, what feels unfair, what you are tired of pretending is fine, or what you wish someone understood. You do not have to solve it right now — you only have to let it be real.

Vent spaces work best when they are honest and private. Do not filter your feelings into something “acceptable.” The goal is not to sound mature; the goal is to feel lighter.
